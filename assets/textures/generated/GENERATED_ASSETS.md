# Generated texture set

These 512×512 mobile-ready textures were created with OpenAI's built-in image-generation model for Dinos4Helena. They are used as color textures with triplanar projection, which keeps them usable on both procedural geometry and source models that have no UV map.

## Prompt set

All prompts requested a perfectly tileable, flat orthographic, neutrally lit, realistic hand-painted PBR base-color texture with no object silhouette, text, border, watermark, highlights, or baked shadows.

- `dino_skin_olive.png`: fine overlapping dinosaur scales; olive green and muted ochre mottling; subtle dark irregular stripes.
- `dino_skin_russet.png`: small pebbled scales; burnt sienna, clay red, and dark umber mottling; sparse charcoal bands.
- `dino_skin_sand.png`: fine leathery scales; sandy beige, muted cream, dusty brown speckles, and subtle broken stripes.
- `bark_dark_moss.png`: rugged ancient conifer bark; deep vertical fissures; dark warm brown with mossy olive traces.
- `bark_pale_lichen.png`: weathered prehistoric bark; broad fibrous ridges, shallow cracks, grey-brown/russet color, and pale lichen.
- `rock_basalt_moss.png`: dark weathered basalt; charcoal fractured mineral grain and sparse muted-green moss flecks.
- `rock_sandstone.png`: warm sandstone/limestone; layered ochre, tan, grey mineral variation, erosion, and fine cracks.
- `ground_forest_loam.png`: damp dark forest loam; decomposed leaf fragments, tiny fern fragments, and sparse moss.
- `ground_grassland.png`: short muted-green grassland; brown soil patches and scattered dry blades.
- `ground_floodplain.png`: muted ochre silt and pale sand; fine dark grains and subtle dried-mud cracks.
- `ground_desert_sand.png`: wind-rippled warm ochre sand, tiny pebbles, and subtle dried-sediment cracks.
- `ground_winter_snow.png`: windswept powder snow, icy granules, and pale blue-grey compacted patches.
- `ground_volcanic_basalt.png`: cracked charcoal basalt, cooled ropy lava crust, ash, and restrained mineral-orange fissures.

The original generator outputs were 1254×1254 PNG files. Project copies were resized to 512×512 and stripped of metadata to reduce Android download and GPU memory cost.
